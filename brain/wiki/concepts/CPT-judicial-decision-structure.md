# CPT-judicial-decision-structure

## What it means

The compositional and discursive organisation of judicial decision texts — recurring procedural stages, participant roles and function patterns — including how far procedural law fixes, or leaves open, a template for judicial acts. A candidate concept coined during the ingest of SRC-0008 and drawn from its claims [CLM-0008-002] [CLM-0008-011] [CLM-0008-012] [CLM-0008-013] [CLM-0008-014].

## Claims

### Descriptive

**RU**

- In tax decisions of the Russian Supreme Court, the common legal logic primarily follows the 'cause → legal_ref → conclusion' pattern — the most frequent three-element function chain — and most discourse functions are linked by the cause function, which confirms its structural importance in legal reasoning as the main connector. — rests on factual basis (SRC-0008). [CLM-0008-002]
- There is no single mandatory template for judicial decisions in Russian procedural law: the structure and content requirements for judicial acts are regulated by the Civil Procedure Code of the Russian Federation, the Arbitration Procedure Code and other legal codes, but this does not imply the existence of a rigidly fixed template with immutable fields. — rests on legal sources (SRC-0008). [CLM-0008-011]
- Empirical reconstruction of the invariant structure of judicial decisions identifies elements present in the overwhelming majority of texts despite the absence of formal requirements: the stages of preparatory, consideration, debate and decision correspond to the actual structure of Russian judicial acts, even though the law does not require explicit marking of these sections. — rests on factual basis (SRC-0008). [CLM-0008-012]
- In Russian Supreme Court tax decisions, the distribution of discursive functions is subordinate to the role of the participant in the process: claimants typically initiate disputes through 'elaboration → contrast' sequences, defendants tend to use the 'evidence → fact' pattern, other courts whose decisions are reviewed generally follow the 'legal_ref → cause' logic, and the Supreme Court most often uses the 'contrast → legal_ref' pattern. — rests on factual basis (SRC-0008). [CLM-0008-013]
- In Russian Supreme Court tax decisions, parts of the text relating to the Supreme Court have on average the highest number of units per document, suggesting that the court's reasoning has a more 'dense' logical structure; the court's arguments are the most valuable part for discursive analysis, as they contain the most meaningful fragments of reasoning. — rests on factual basis (SRC-0008). [CLM-0008-014]

### Interpretative

**EU**

- For annotation purposes a Judicial Interpretative Formula may be defined as an interpretative statement by the Court, either formulated for the first time or drawn from cited case law; the paragraphs identified as JIFs are those containing interpretation of a rule or general principle, consequences stemming from its interpretation or application, subsumption of a fact within a rule, or qualification of a fact as a concept contained within a rule, and the annotation unit is one paragraph, reflecting the typical structure of CJEU judgments, organised in numbered paragraphs each expressing an autonomous step of reasoning. — rests on abstract considerations (SRC-0009). [CLM-0009-005]
