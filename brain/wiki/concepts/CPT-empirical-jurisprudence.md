# CPT-empirical-jurisprudence

## What it means

The approach that treats the meaning of legal text as an empirical fact discoverable through investigation of real language use — for instance via surveys, corpus linguistics or language models — rather than as a normative construction. A candidate concept coined during the ingest of SRC-0019 and drawn from its claims [CLM-0019-002] [CLM-0019-004] [CLM-0019-006] [CLM-0019-007].

## Claims

### Interpretative

**US**

- The determination of a legal text's ordinary meaning is not an empirical project: ordinary meaning is a value-laden construct defined in part by the interpreter's judgment of what is reasonable, and textualist analysis of ordinary meaning is no more empirical or fact-based than the judicial search for congressional intent. — rests on abstract considerations (SRC-0019). [CLM-0019-002]
- Pushed too far, the very aspect of textualism that seems to promise greater democratic legitimacy — decision-making based on an empirical investigation of ordinary people's understanding of legal text — undermines the interpretive method's political legitimacy; the problem is general to all textualist interpretation but becomes more visible when large language models are used for textualist analysis. — rests on abstract considerations (SRC-0019). [CLM-0019-004]
- The proof of concept offered for asking GPT the ordinary meaning of statutory terms (Engel and McAdams) does not look like an empirical exploration: the LLM inquiries seem to have been engineered to carefully reconstruct results previously obtained from an empirical survey of actual humans, with the outcome viewed as objectively correct chosen in advance. — rests on literature (SRC-0019). [CLM-0019-006]
- Meaning in law, as distinguished from meaning in fact, is a disembodied meaning derived from the community; LLM-based generative interpretation of contracts is just that, derived digitally rather than through the analog musings of a judge or jury, and the data it operates on is community meaning on a vastly larger scale — millions or billions of instances of community word usage rather than the particular usages of the contract parties. — rests on abstract considerations (SRC-0025). [CLM-0025-007]

### Predictive

**US**

- Given the prevalence of AI hype, the use of large language models threatens to exacerbate textualism's 'scientific' quality, suggesting reproducibility and neutrality while obscuring the ever-present normative choices that go into legal interpretation. — rests on abstract considerations (SRC-0019). [CLM-0019-007]
