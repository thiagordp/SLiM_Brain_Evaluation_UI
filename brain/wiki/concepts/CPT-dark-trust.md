# CPT-dark-trust

## What it means

An expectation of predictable behavior about which human beings are rarely if ever conscious, which they barely notice and have no means to measure, and which defies explanation by way of science or legal theory; the unnoticed trust underlying the ability to cope with the world and to contract. A candidate concept coined during the ingest of SRC-0025 and drawn from its claims [CLM-0025-015] [CLM-0025-016].

## Claims

### Interpretative

**general**

- A fourth, contrarian account of contracting: contracts are meaningful because people think they are and rely on them like monkey-ropes, even if that reliance is something of a delusion — contracts, rather than being very meaningful or not nearly as meaningful as one thought, are means of groping for order, reassurance, fulfillment, and certainty in the face of chaos, insecurity, and the unknown unknowns; they can be delusions of order. — rests on abstract considerations (SRC-0025). [CLM-0025-015]
- Humans operate within a universe of social norms the vast majority of which is 'dark trust' — an expectation of predictable behavior about which human beings are rarely if ever conscious, and which defies explanation by way of science or legal theory; the very ability to cope with the world entails dark trust that people barely notice and do not have the means to measure. — rests on abstract considerations (SRC-0025). [CLM-0025-016]
