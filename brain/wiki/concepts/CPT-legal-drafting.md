# CPT-legal-drafting

## What it means

The legal task of producing legal text — legislation, contracts, submissions — rather than analysing it. An anchor concept, given in advance in the grid of `schema/concept.md`; the corpus uses it as the claims below show [CLM-0010-001] [CLM-0010-006] [CLM-0010-007].

## Claims

### Descriptive

**BR+US**

- Real-life legislators have already used ChatGPT to draft entire bills: a legislative act written by ChatGPT was adopted by the Porto Alegre city council in Brazil in November 2023, and a Massachusetts state senator turned to ChatGPT to help write a bill regulating artificial intelligence models; at least one of those bills has passed and is now part of a legal system, and these cases are representative of a starting worldwide trend. — rests on factual basis (SRC-0010). [CLM-0010-007]

**general**

- In the philosophical debate on generative artificial intelligence and law, most attention has gone to the normative question whether AI should create and interpret law, while the prior question whether AI can create and interpret law — whether AI is capable of producing outputs that can be deemed law, and whether AI can be a legal author — has remained largely unattended. — rests on literature (SRC-0010). [CLM-0010-001]
- Even without legal fine-tuning, commercially available large language models can generate legal-like texts hardly distinguishable from human-created legal texts, although their outputs are overly generic and heavily dependent on the abilities of the human prompter. — rests on factual basis (SRC-0010). [CLM-0010-006]
- The gap between legal interpretation and formal logic is not unique to contract entailment: it arises wherever AI systems cite sources to support legal claims, which is increasingly common in LLM-assisted drafting, regulatory analysis, and litigation support. — rests on literature (SRC-0022). [CLM-0022-013]

### Interpretative

**general**

- The practice of making contracts has less to do with the logic of the text than it does with ritual, self-actualization, and the unrequited human desire for certainty; contracts are rituals that help avoid violence as a means of conflict resolution and serve higher orders of self-fulfillment. — rests on abstract considerations (SRC-0025). [CLM-0025-004]
