# CPT-copyright-infringement-risk

## What it means

The risk that collecting or using data or works, for instance in AI training or commercial software development, infringes copyright, as shaped by jurisdiction-specific doctrines and exceptions such as fair use, fair dealing and text-and-data-mining provisions. A candidate concept coined during the ingest of SRC-0012 and drawn from its claims [CLM-0012-004].

## Claims

### Descriptive

**US+GB+CA+EU**

- Data contained in publicly available datasets may be copyright-protected and unauthorized commercial use can constitute infringement; while the Fair Use doctrine in the United States permits certain uses without permission if they do not cause material harm to the copyright holder, jurisdictions such as the UK, Canada and the EU, through fair dealing exceptions and directives like the EU's Text and Data Mining Directive, typically restrict such uses to non-commercial purposes without explicit consent, so using datasets with copyrighted content for commercial AI-powered software development can lead to legal challenges depending on the jurisdiction. — rests on legal sources (SRC-0012). [CLM-0012-004]
