# CPT-judicial-interpretative-formulas

## What it means

Interpretative statements a court formulates or draws from cited case law — in CJEU practice developed over time through self-citation — whose value extends beyond the specific case, and which differ from the ratio decidendi in not being limited to the elements necessary to reach the court's conclusion. A candidate concept coined during the ingest of SRC-0009 and drawn from its claims [CLM-0009-001] [CLM-0009-002] [CLM-0009-005].

## Claims

### Descriptive

**EU**

- Manually extracting Judicial Interpretative Formulas (JIFs) from decisions of the Court of Justice of the European Union on Value Added Tax is effortful, automatic extraction had not previously been investigated in the VAT domain, and a pipeline method for automated JIF extraction in this domain is the first of its kind. — rests on literature (SRC-0009). [CLM-0009-001]

### Interpretative

**EU**

- The Court of Justice of the European Union adopts an argumentative style with broad interpretative statements — Judicial Interpretative Formulas, legal texts or standards the CJEU develops over time through self-citation — whose value extends beyond specific issues as they are progressively cited and integrated into EU law; these differ from ratio decidendi in that they are not limited to the elements necessary to reach the Court's conclusion. — rests on literature (SRC-0009). [CLM-0009-002]
- For annotation purposes a Judicial Interpretative Formula may be defined as an interpretative statement by the Court, either formulated for the first time or drawn from cited case law; the paragraphs identified as JIFs are those containing interpretation of a rule or general principle, consequences stemming from its interpretation or application, subsumption of a fact within a rule, or qualification of a fact as a concept contained within a rule, and the annotation unit is one paragraph, reflecting the typical structure of CJEU judgments, organised in numbered paragraphs each expressing an autonomous step of reasoning. — rests on abstract considerations (SRC-0009). [CLM-0009-005]
