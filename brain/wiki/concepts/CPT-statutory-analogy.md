# CPT-statutory-analogy

## What it means

The canon that extends a statutory rule to a case the text does not cover, on the ground that the case is relevantly similar to those the rule governs. An anchor concept, given in advance in the grid of `schema/concept.md`; the corpus uses it as the claims below show [CLM-0026-015].

## Claims

### Descriptive

**general**

- In cases of a lacuna in law, AI must be able to reason via analogies from related provisions or derive a solution from the general principles that underpin that area of law; adjudicating without the support of a rule requires a deep, systemic understanding of the entire legal framework, and while an LLM can process vast amounts of text, it struggles to track the dependencies and interactions across many factors, such as laws and principles simultaneously — a critical point of failure for AI. — rests on literature (SRC-0026).
