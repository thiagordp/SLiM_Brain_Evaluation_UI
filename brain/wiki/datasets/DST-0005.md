# DST-0005 — evaluation dataset of 814 CPT codes across 7 coverage documents

## Papers and claims

SRC-0007 introduces this dataset and uses it for its headline efficiency and effectiveness result [CLM-0007-002], the finetuned-versus-zero-shot retriever comparison [CLM-0007-005], the cost comparison between symbolic rule-based inference and LLM-based inference [CLM-0007-007], the performance-versus-cost tradeoff between LLMs on retrieved text and rule-based approaches [CLM-0007-008], the retrieved-passages-versus-entire-document comparison [CLM-0007-009], the error analysis of rule failures [CLM-0007-010], and the traceability illustration contrasting rule-based reasoning with direct prompting [CLM-0007-011].

## Language and jurisdiction

The texts are plain-text coverage documents and procedure descriptions presented in English; the paper does not name the legal system they belong to [CLM-0007-002].

## Annotation

The dataset carries human-generated coverage determinations, which serve as ground truth for accuracy and F1 evaluation; the paper does not describe the annotation method for these determinations and reports no inter-annotator agreement [CLM-0007-002].
