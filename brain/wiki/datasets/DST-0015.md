# DST-0015 — 20 consultation queries about complex marriage situations

## Papers and claims

SRC-0014 introduces this query set for its user study and rests four claims on it: that visually presenting the legal-article basis of each response sentence helps users understand and verify the advice, accurately provided for about 95% of queries [CLM-0014-004]; that interactive selection of legal articles turns largely imperfect top-3 retrievals into correct responses in 80% of cases [CLM-0014-005]; that retrieved legal cases with highlighted relevant sentences benefited 77% of queries and streamline reading [CLM-0014-006]; and that judicial interpretations assist comprehension where interpretation is required [CLM-0014-009].

## Language and jurisdiction

The queries concern complex marriage situations under Chinese law, answered against articles of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China and China's judicial interpretations [CLM-0014-005] [CLM-0014-009].

## Annotation and agreement

The queries are evaluated by 3 invited non-legal professional users reporting whether each module helped their consultation; no inter-rater agreement is reported [CLM-0014-005].
