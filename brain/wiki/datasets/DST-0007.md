# DST-0007 — corpus of 101 CJEU preliminary rulings on VAT annotated for Judicial Interpretative Formulas (jif-cjeu)

## Papers and claims

SRC-0009 introduces this dataset [CLM-0009-014] and rests on it the inter-annotator agreement result for its annotation guidelines [CLM-0009-006], the selection of DeepSeek-R1 as the best LLM annotator [CLM-0009-007], the finding that BERT-based models fine-tuned on LLM-annotated data perform comparably to LLMs [CLM-0009-008], the speculation that these models inherited the annotator's tendency to favour false positives [CLM-0009-009], the suggestion that lexical cues play a crucial role in the task [CLM-0009-010], the judgement that LEGAL-BERT is the best model [CLM-0009-011], and the conclusion that LLM-produced annotations can distil LLM knowledge into smaller comparable models [CLM-0009-012].

## Language and jurisdiction

The texts are preliminary rulings of the Court of Justice of the European Union in the VAT domain, so their jurisdiction is the EU [CLM-0009-014]. The annotated portions are delimited by English section markers of the judgments ('Consideration of the question referred', 'Costs') [CLM-0009-005].

## Annotation and agreement

21 decisions were manually annotated by legal experts under iteratively refined guidelines, and 80 were annotated automatically by DeepSeek-R1, the best of three LLMs evaluated against the manually annotated validation split [CLM-0009-007] [CLM-0009-014]. Inter-annotator agreement on the independently annotated set C, at paragraph level and restricted to the relevant portions of the judgments, was Cohen's kappa 0.96, almost perfect agreement [CLM-0009-006].
