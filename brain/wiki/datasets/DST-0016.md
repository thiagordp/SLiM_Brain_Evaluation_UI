# DST-0016 — SIRAC

Introduced by [SRC-0015](../sources/SRC-0015.md), which presents it as the first semi-structured IRAC corpus [CLM-0015-001]. Its texts are English-language legal scenarios pertaining to the Contract Act Malaysia (MY) and the Australian Social Act (AU) [CLM-0015-001].

SRC-0015's claims rest on it for the construction of the corpus itself [CLM-0015-001], the baseline evaluation of ChatGPT's answers and reasoning paths [CLM-0015-003], the effect of feeding human-written reasoning paths [CLM-0015-004], in-context learning [CLM-0015-005], question decomposition [CLM-0015-006], the fluency and reference-quality findings [CLM-0015-007], the overall alignment conclusion [CLM-0015-008], the assumption-evaluation agreement figures [CLM-0015-009], and the assumption-identification improvements [CLM-0015-010].

Annotation: five senior law students, selected by interview and trained on test cases verified by law professors, annotated all scenarios with complete IRAC analyses under detailed guidelines [CLM-0015-001]. Agreement was reported for the human evaluation of ChatGPT outputs conducted on the corpus: Cohen's Kappa of 0.55 over all evaluation measures, 0.75 without the assumption evaluation [CLM-0015-009].
