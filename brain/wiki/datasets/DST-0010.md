# DST-0010 — LLeQA

## Papers and claims

SRC-0011 uses this dataset for its cross-lingual robustness result: the SyLeR framework achieves the best performance among compared methods on LLeQA even with the tree-based retrieval module omitted and replaced by BM25 retrieval of the most relevant statute [CLM-0011-015].

## Language and jurisdiction

The texts are French-language legal questions with expert-annotated answers, and the associated corpus consists of French legal statutes; the paper using the dataset treats "French" as a language dimension and does not name the legal system the texts belong to, so the jurisdiction is undetermined [CLM-0011-015].

## Annotation and agreement

The questions are expert-annotated according to the paper that uses the dataset; no annotation procedure or inter-annotator agreement is reported there [CLM-0011-015].
