# DST-0011 — Dataset Licenses (DL) dataset

## Papers and claims

SRC-0012 introduces this dataset and uses it for its evaluation of existing legal foundation models on dataset license compliance analysis [CLM-0012-006], the finding that general-purpose foundation models produce semantically similar but inaccurate responses [CLM-0012-007], the headline result that LicenseGPT, fine-tuned on the dataset, significantly outperforms legal and general-purpose foundation models [CLM-0012-008], the prompt-sensitivity analysis [CLM-0012-011], and the fine-tuning data-size analysis showing diminishing returns [CLM-0012-012].

## Language and jurisdiction

The texts are licenses of publicly available datasets collected from 10 international dataset hosting platforms; the paper does not state the language of the licenses or the legal system they belong to, so the jurisdiction is undetermined [CLM-0012-006].

## Annotation and agreement

Four software IP lawyers, with 8, 10, 15 and 20 years of experience, independently labeled each license for commercial usability and documented rights and obligations or reasons, with final labels settled in a joint discussion; no inter-annotator agreement statistic is reported [CLM-0012-008]. The paper states the dataset is proprietary and was not open-sourced with the model [CLM-0012-008].
