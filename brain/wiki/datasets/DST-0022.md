# DST-0022 — Bias Detector Quiz (BDQ) and Bias Compensator Quiz (BCQ)

Introduced by [SRC-0021](../sources/SRC-0021.md): multiple-choice contamination test sets derived from SARA, in which a model must select the option matching the original SARA instance, with positional bias estimated and corrected via chance-adjusted accuracy [CLM-0021-001]. The underlying texts are English-language SARA instances about US tax law, so their jurisdiction is the US [CLM-0021-001].

## Papers and claims

SRC-0021 uses these test sets for its claim that recent LLM performance in the legal domain is partly inflated by data contamination and benchmark memorization [CLM-0021-001], for the finding that newer frontier models exhibit higher contamination than earlier models [CLM-0021-002], and for the finding that contamination is strongly associated with direct question-answering performance but only weakly with Prolog-based performance [CLM-0021-003].

## Annotation

The perturbed options are generated by an LLM under meaning-preservation constraints; no human annotation process or inter-annotator agreement is reported for these quiz sets [CLM-0021-001].
