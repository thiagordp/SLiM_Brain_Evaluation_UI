# DST-0012 — TikTok privacy policy

## Papers and claims

SRC-0013 uses this policy text, together with DST-0013, as evaluation input for its extraction result of 974 distinct data practice edges from an approximately 15,000-word policy [CLM-0013-011], for its semantic-search tractability and sub-linear scaling result across 23 verification queries [CLM-0013-012], and for the finding that full-policy first-order logic formulas remain too complex for SMT solvers without search-space reduction [CLM-0013-013].
