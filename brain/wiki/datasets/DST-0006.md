# DST-0006 — 100 tax-related decisions of the Russian Supreme Court (sudact.ru)

Introduced by [SRC-0008](../sources/SRC-0008.md), which built it to study the discursive structure of legal reasoning. Its texts are Russian-language (ru) judicial decisions of the Supreme Court of the Russian Federation (jurisdiction RU), collected from the open database sudact.ru.

## Papers and claims resting on it

SRC-0008 rests the following claims on this dataset: the dominant 'cause → legal_ref → conclusion' pattern of legal logic [CLM-0008-002]; the ineffectiveness of elementary discourse units for legal-text markup [CLM-0008-007]; the AI-human agreement levels for stages, roles and functions [CLM-0008-008]; the difficulty of function annotation even for experts [CLM-0008-009]; the reproducibility of automatic LLM annotation across runs [CLM-0008-010]; the empirically invariant four-stage structure of judicial decisions [CLM-0008-012]; the subordination of discursive function patterns to participant roles [CLM-0008-013]; and the density of the Supreme Court's reasoning [CLM-0008-014].

## Annotation and agreement

The whole corpus was annotated automatically by ChatGPT-4.1 with few-shot prompting at temperature zero, and ten texts were annotated manually by two independent expert annotators as benchmarks [CLM-0008-008]. Reported agreement: AI-human 97.31% on stages, 65.59% on roles, 53.76% on functions [CLM-0008-008]; human-human agreement on functions 40.65% [CLM-0008-009]; stability of automatic annotation across five iterations, average Cohen's Kappa 0.82 [CLM-0008-010].
