# DST-0003 — LIQA (legal interpretative question-answer) dataset

Used by SRC-0004, which introduced it, for its evaluation of six zero-shot Chain-of-Thought prompt variants for long-context-window LLM provision retrieval [CLM-0004-004], for the robustness [CLM-0004-005], navigation [CLM-0004-006] and execution-time [CLM-0004-007] effects of a high-level community summary, for the model's referencing of definition provisions [CLM-0004-008], for the suggestion of predefining top related provisions [CLM-0004-010], and for the argument that the underlying MOLEG document is well-suited as ground truth [CLM-0004-011].

The texts are Korean building code interpretation cases from South Korea (KR) [CLM-0004-011]. The cases were selected by the Ministry of Government Legislation, whose responses and reasoning serve as ground-truth answers; the cases require interpreting the meaning and relationships between provisions, as the answers are not explicitly stated in the legal texts [CLM-0004-011]. No human annotation beyond the official MOLEG responses, and no inter-annotator agreement, is reported [CLM-0004-011].
