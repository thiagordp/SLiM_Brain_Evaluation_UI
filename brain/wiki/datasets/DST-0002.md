# DST-0002 — set of 19 progressively complex questions covering key aspects of the EU VAT Directive

## Papers and claims

SRC-0002 introduces this dataset — 19 progressively complex legal questions on the EU VAT Directive, created by a legal expert — and uses it for its overall truthfulness result across nine LLMs [CLM-0002-001], the ranking of models by expert-scored truthfulness [CLM-0002-002], the scaling finding for Llama 3.1 405B versus 8B [CLM-0002-003], the variability of Llama 3.1 8B and its fine-tuning potential [CLM-0002-004], the within-family versus cross-family agreement finding [CLM-0002-005], the highest-agreement pair GPT-4o and Llama 3.1 405B [CLM-0002-006], the question-difficulty observations [CLM-0002-007], the misalignment of models on exceptional cases in the law [CLM-0002-008], and the concluding call for standardized benchmarks, human oversight and domain-specific fine-tuning [CLM-0002-009].

## Language and jurisdiction

The questions concern the EU VAT Directive (Council Directive 2006/112/EC), so their jurisdiction is the EU [CLM-0002-001]. They are presented in English in the paper [CLM-0002-001].

## Annotation

A legal expert from the Hellenic State Legal Council created the 19 questions, and the same legal expert scored each anonymized LLM response for truthfulness on a scale from one to ten [CLM-0002-001] [CLM-0002-002]. No inter-annotator agreement is reported; agreement statistics (Cohen's Kappa) are reported between the models' responses, not between annotators [CLM-0002-006].
