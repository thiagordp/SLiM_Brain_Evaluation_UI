# DST-0024 — 1,143 English-language Instagram posts by Dutch registered influencers

## Papers and claims

SRC-0023 introduces this dataset and uses it for its classification results: the overall performance of gpt-5-nano and gemini-2.5-flash-lite and the performance drop on the 95 ambiguous posts [CLM-0023-004], the finding that adding regulatory text to the prompt does not consistently improve detection accuracy [CLM-0023-005], the inference that LLMs rely on pretraining patterns rather than the provided legal text [CLM-0023-006], the model-choice profile of recall-oriented gemini-2.5-flash-lite versus precision-oriented gpt-5-nano [CLM-0023-009], the comparison of LLMs against a TF-IDF logistic regression baseline [CLM-0023-010], and the concluding prescription to pair performance metrics with legal-reasoning audits [CLM-0023-013].

## Language and jurisdiction

The posts are written in English by influencers registered in the Dutch Video-Uploader Registry, and disclosure sufficiency is assessed against the Dutch Advertising Code, so the jurisdiction is the Netherlands [CLM-0023-004] [CLM-0023-012].

## Annotation

Posts without legally sufficient disclosures were annotated by three domain experts distinguishing hidden advertisements from organic content: two annotators had to reach consensus, with disagreements or uncertain cases referred to a third; excluding 10.34% uncertain cases, the first two annotators achieved a 92.64% absolute agreement rate and 0.74 Krippendorff's Alpha [CLM-0023-004].
