# DST-0014 — LeCaRD

## Papers and claims

SRC-0014 uses this external dataset to fine-tune the BGE embedding model into its legal case retrieval model and to evaluate it: fine-tuned BGE significantly increases each NDCG@K over BM25 and non-fine-tuned BGE on the LeCaRD test set, while specialised case-retrieval models still outperform it [CLM-0014-007].

## Language and jurisdiction

The texts are Chinese legal cases; the dataset is described as a legal case retrieval dataset for the Chinese law system [CLM-0014-007].

## Annotation and agreement

SRC-0014 describes it only as a publicly available Chinese legal case retrieval dataset and reports no annotation method or agreement statistic for it [CLM-0014-007].
