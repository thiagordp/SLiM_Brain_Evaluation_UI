# DST-0001 — 45 real-world DPAs

## Papers and claims

SRC-0001 uses this dataset for its comparative evaluation of GPT-4o and GPT-5.1-thinking on GDPR compliance checking of Data Processing Agreements [CLM-0001-010] [CLM-0001-011], for the overall performance claim of its prompt-driven framework [CLM-0001-002], for the contention that prompt design rather than context size drives accuracy gains [CLM-0001-003], and for the concluding claim that carefully designed prompts can serve as the primary mechanism for guiding legal reasoning in LLM-based compliance systems [CLM-0001-013].

## Language and jurisdiction

The texts are Data Processing Agreements assessed against the GDPR, so their jurisdiction is the EU [CLM-0001-002]. The language of the texts is not stated by the paper that uses the dataset [CLM-0001-002].

## Annotation

The paper reports that each DPA was independently annotated by three legal experts against 12 core GDPR compliance rules derived from Articles 28, 32, 33 and 44, yielding 10,704 labeled SCU-rule pairs with strong inter-annotator agreement (Fleiss' kappa = 0.82) [CLM-0001-010].
