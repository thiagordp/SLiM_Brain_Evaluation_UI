# DST-0021 — SARA +

Introduced by [SRC-0021](../sources/SRC-0021.md): a synthetic evaluation suite built from SARA to minimize data contamination while preserving legal and structural complexity, with formal Prolog annotations and exact solutions generated without human expert effort [CLM-0021-010]. The texts are English-language perturbations of SARA's US Internal Revenue Code statutes and cases, so their jurisdiction is the US [CLM-0021-010].

## Papers and claims

SRC-0021 uses the suite for its central contribution claim [CLM-0021-010], for the finding that direct question-answering performance drops sharply under case and rule perturbations while Prolog-based performance remains stable [CLM-0021-006], for the finding that monolithic large language models are relatively robust to linguistic variation [CLM-0021-007], for the conclusion that large language models are more reliable as translators into formal logic than as standalone reasoners [CLM-0021-005], and for its advocacy of hybrid neuro-symbolic systems [CLM-0021-008].

## Annotation

The perturbed rules and a subset of the perturbed cases are manually verified for quality control, and identical perturbations are applied to textual rules and Prolog programs so the formal problem and its solutions stay aligned [CLM-0021-010]. No inter-annotator agreement is reported [CLM-0021-010].
