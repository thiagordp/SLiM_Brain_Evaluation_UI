# DST-0018 — 56 German court decisions related to § 6 StVO

## Papers and claims

SRC-0017 uses this dataset for its comparative evaluation of four prompt engineering techniques with GPT-4o on zero-shot extraction of normative sentences [CLM-0017-006], for the observation that legal language complexity keeps GPT-4o from consistently extracting the most relevant normative statements [CLM-0017-007], for the finding that GPT-4o-generated Chain-of-Instructions prompts are inconsistent and weaker [CLM-0017-008], for the finding that layered task decomposition with Standard prompts in early layers and Chain-of-Instructions in the final layer enhances effectiveness [CLM-0017-009], and for the contention that reducing the review workload from 6,242 sentences to 560 would significantly streamline manual review [CLM-0017-010].

## Language and jurisdiction

The texts are German court decisions concerning § 6 of the German Road Traffic Regulation (Straßenverkehrsordnung, StVO), so the language is German and the jurisdiction is Germany [CLM-0017-006].

## Annotation

Qualified legal experts searched the relevant case law in the juris and beck-online databases and an extensive collection of traffic law, and the gold normative sentences were identified through manual expert review of the court decisions [CLM-0017-002]. Of the 6,242 sentences in the 56 decisions, 125 were identified as normative and specifically related to § 6 StVO [CLM-0017-006]. No inter-annotator agreement is reported by the paper that uses the dataset [CLM-0017-002].
