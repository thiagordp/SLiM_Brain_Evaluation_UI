# DST-0017 — SARA

External dataset, English-language, with scenarios pertaining to the US Internal Revenue Code.

## Papers and claims

[SRC-0015](../sources/SRC-0015.md) uses its 276 IRC scenarios to evaluate ChatGPT's final answers, on which ChatGPT scored an F1 of 0.35 — the lowest of the three areas of law evaluated [CLM-0015-003]. SRC-0015 also characterises it, in contrasting SIRAC with existing datasets, as codifying reasoning paths in Prolog, which is challenging for legal professionals to understand [CLM-0015-002].

[SRC-0021](../sources/SRC-0021.md) uses SARA as its statutory tax reasoning benchmark: for the contamination findings — that recent LLM performance on legal reasoning benchmarks is partly inflated by data contamination [CLM-0021-001], that newer frontier models exhibit higher contamination on SARA than earlier models [CLM-0021-002], and that measured contamination is strongly associated with direct question-answering performance while the correlation with Prolog-based performance is weak [CLM-0021-003]; for the comparison of Prolog-based reasoning against direct question answering [CLM-0021-004] and the reading of LLMs as translators rather than standalone reasoners [CLM-0021-005]; for the perturbation-robustness findings [CLM-0021-006] and the neuro-symbolic reliability conclusion [CLM-0021-008]; for the entailment-versus-numerical difficulty finding [CLM-0021-009]; for the construction of the SARA + synthetic suite from it [CLM-0021-010] (see [DST-0021](DST-0021.md)); and for the readings of prior SARA results [CLM-0021-011] and of model recency and scale [CLM-0021-012].

The two papers describe the dataset differently, and the record keeps the description written when it entered the graph: SRC-0021 attributes it to Holzenberger, Blair-Stanek and Van Durme (2020, NLLP workshop) rather than Holzenberger and Van Durme (2021), counts 276 entailment plus 100 numeric instances rather than 276 scenarios, and stresses its human-annotated Prolog Horn clauses [CLM-0021-004] [CLM-0021-010]; the divergence is logged in wiki/log.md.

## Language and jurisdiction

The scenarios concern the US Internal Revenue Code, so the jurisdiction is the United States [CLM-0015-003] [CLM-0021-004]. The texts are English [CLM-0015-002].

## Annotation

SRC-0015 reports no annotation process or inter-annotator agreement for SARA itself, noting only that it contains no human-annotated natural-language reasoning paths [CLM-0015-002]. SRC-0021 describes the dataset's statutes and cases as carrying human-annotated Prolog Horn clauses, with no agreement figures reported [CLM-0021-010].
