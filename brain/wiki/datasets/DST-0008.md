# DST-0008 — Practitioner

## Papers and claims

SRC-0011 uses this dataset, together with DST-0009, for its headline result that the SyLeR framework achieves the best performance across all metrics against legal-specific and open-domain baselines [CLM-0011-010], the attribution of legal LLMs' strength to embedded legal knowledge [CLM-0011-011], the finding that fine-tuning-based reasoning enhancement outperforms prompt-based methods [CLM-0011-012], the ablation of SyLeR's three components [CLM-0011-013], and the cross-domain generalization result between practitioner and layperson user groups [CLM-0011-014].

## Language and jurisdiction

The texts are Chinese-language questions and answers from the subjective sections of the National Unified Legal Professional Qualification Examination, so their jurisdiction is China (CN) [CLM-0011-010]. The dataset is derived from the LexEval benchmark and represents questions from legal professionals with detailed case descriptions [CLM-0011-010].

## Annotation and agreement

The answers are described as manually annotated answers collected from the examination's subjective sections; the paper using it reports no further annotation and no inter-annotator agreement for this dataset [CLM-0011-010].
