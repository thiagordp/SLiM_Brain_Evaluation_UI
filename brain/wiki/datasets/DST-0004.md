# DST-0004 — expert-labeled (CPT, subsection, relevance) pairs

## Papers and claims

SRC-0007 introduces this dataset and uses it, together with DST-0005, for the claim that a cross-encoder retriever finetuned on these expert-annotated pairs consistently outperforms a zero-shot retriever, improving accuracy by an average of 2.69% and F1 score by 1.72% [CLM-0007-005].

## Language and jurisdiction

The texts are subsections of Certificates of Coverage/Summary Plan Documents, presented in English in the paper; the paper does not name the legal system the coverage documents belong to [CLM-0007-005].

## Annotation

Approximately 20 certified coding Subject Matter Experts, engaged through an internal annotation platform with an arbitration process to ensure consistency, selected for each CPT code only the passages that decide coverage; no inter-annotator agreement statistic is reported [CLM-0007-005].
